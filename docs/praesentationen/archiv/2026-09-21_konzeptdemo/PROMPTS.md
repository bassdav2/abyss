## 01-proben-sammeln

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE wide 16:9 concept gameplay screenshot for ABYSS, part of a coherent presentation explaining a small 2D research-submarine game. The supplied image is the ART-DIRECTION AND SUBMARINE DESIGN REFERENCE, not an image to merely reproduce. Preserve its exact readable side-view cutaway hull, four compartments (rear engine/battery, pump room, research laboratory, forward cockpit), warm amber interior, German technical typography and restrained dark metal HUD. No new crew, combat, inventory systems or multiplayer.

NEW SCENE: EXPLORATION AND SAMPLE COLLECTION, before the emergency. Show a substantially different, wider composition: the intact, completely dry submarine floats in the upper middle-left, occupying about 50% of the frame width, above a stepped rocky seabed with tall basalt formations and sparse turquoise bioluminescent organisms. The forward searchlight and a down-facing collection light illuminate a small mineral sample in the lower center-right. A simple articulated research sampling arm from underneath the vessel is visibly reaching toward this sample; make this readable and plausible, with a small sampling claw. Use clear 2D side-view geometry and painterly atmosphere matching the reference. Show a graceful trail of bubbles, soft blue shafts of light, and enough brighter local light to read the sample interaction.

German UI, clean and minimal:
Top left "ABYSS" and "MISSION 02 · TIEFENPROBE".
Top strip "TIEFE 248 m", "ENERGIE 76 %", "PROBEN 1 / 3".
Top right calm green status "SYSTEME STABIL".
One restrained callout near the illuminated sample reads "MINERALPROBE" with a small action button "PROBE BERGEN".
The research compartment shows sample storage, without excessive detail.
Bottom console uses the same ANTRIEB / PUMPEN / BALLAST / SCHOTT modules as the reference, with ANTRIEB at 0 %, PUMPEN at 10 %, BALLAST at 50 %, SCHOTT labeled OFFEN. A small sonar shows the submarine and remaining sample targets. Optional short status line "POSITION HALTEN".
No modal dialogs, no enormous titles, no screenshot border or computer frame, no explanatory marketing copy.
The picture must instantly communicate the first gameplay loop: explore, locate a sample, stop and collect it while managing limited energy. This is a visual concept, not a claim of existing working software.
